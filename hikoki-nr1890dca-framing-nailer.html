Hikoki NR1890DCA framing nailer

Published: Sat 03 October 2026
By morganp

In Woodwork.

tags: Hikoki Nail Gun Workshop Tools

The Hikoki NR1890DCA is a second-generation brushless cordless framing nailer powered by an 18V battery.

Nail specifications

Item Specification
Nail length 50 to 90 mm
Shank diameter 2.9 to 3.3 mm
Collation Paper strip
Strip angle 30 to 34 degrees
Head Clipped head, 6.8 to 7.7 mm wide
Shank type Smooth, barbed or ring
Magazine 47 nails
Firing modes Sequential or contact (bump)

The manual excludes screw-type nails.

Using a framing nailer

A framing nailer drives long, thick nails into structural timber. It suits stud walls, joists, rafters, fence rails and timber frames. This model runs on an 18V battery alone, with no compressor, hose or gas cartridge, so the tool moves around a site or garden without set-up time.

Treated timber needs the right nail. The copper in the preservative corrodes the zinc plating on standard galvanised nails. The nails then rust and lose their grip. Use stainless steel, hot-dip galvanised or extra-galvanised nails in treated timber, and choose stainless steel for exposed decking, fencing and coastal sites.
